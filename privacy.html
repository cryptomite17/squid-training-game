Privacy Policy

Squid Training Game ("the app"). Last updated: 16 November 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your play to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your score, settings and hand history are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a subscription is active. Apple's handling of your data is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is a poker study tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
